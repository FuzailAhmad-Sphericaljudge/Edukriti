# Phase 13: Revision Studio

Phase 13 adds a complete post-lesson study surface generated from each personalized lesson.

## Delivered

- Automatic concise notes for every teaching segment, including objectives and recall prompts.
- Downloadable plain-text revision notes that work without an extra service.
- Interactive flashcards with question/answer reveal and keyboard-accessible controls.
- A seven-day plan that progresses through learning, recall, mixed practice, assessment, and the recommended next lesson.
- Revision Studio links from the lesson plan, completed classroom, and final learning report.

## Acceptance checks

- The revision route validates and loads the same durable lesson plan used by the classroom.
- Notes and flashcards reflect the lesson’s actual segments rather than generic placeholder content.
- Download creates a topic-named notes file in the browser.
- The planner always creates seven focused days, including final assessment and next-step guidance.
