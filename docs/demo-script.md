# Edukriti 4-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:30 — The problem and product

“Recorded videos cannot react when a learner is confused, while chatbots rarely teach through a structured lesson. Edukriti is an adaptive AI teacher that turns a topic or document into a personalized, avatar-led class.”

Show the dashboard and select **Start with a topic**.

## 0:30–1:00 — Personalization and planning

Enter `Teach me electricity using simple everyday examples`. Select beginner, Hinglish, and 20 minutes. Generate the lesson and briefly show objectives, timed segments, checkpoint placement, visuals, and the source-grounding badge.

## 1:00–1:50 — AI classroom

Select **Enter AI classroom**. The visual lesson starts immediately. Select **Start teacher voice**, then let Aarohi speak while captions and the Ohm’s Law visual appear. Point out the lesson timeline and subject-aware visual selection.

## 1:50–2:40 — Interaction and adaptation

At the checkpoint enter `Current gets used up by the bulb.` Explain that this is deliberately wrong. Show that Edukriti does more than mark it incorrect: it identifies the current-consumption misconception, gives constructive Hinglish feedback, and switches to a water-pipe analogy. Play the alternate explanation.

## 2:40–3:25 — Assessment and report

Complete the lesson and open the final assessment. Show one MCQ and the short-answer question. Finish the quiz, then show the score, strengths, weak concepts, misconception record, revision actions, and recommended next lesson.

## 3:25–4:00 — Grounding and architecture

Return to the dashboard to show durable progress. Close with: “Edukriti combines document RAG, validated lesson planning, multilingual voice, a human-like avatar, adaptive teaching, and assessment in one complete learning loop—not a chatbot and not a static video.”
