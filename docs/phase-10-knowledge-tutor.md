# Phase 10: Knowledge and tutor refinement

Phase 10 makes the classroom more useful after the lesson starts, not only during lesson generation.

## Delivered

- Expanded the lesson-planning instructions for prerequisite-first teaching, factual caution, misconceptions, concrete examples, visuals, and application checks.
- Increased retrieved lesson evidence from eight to ten ranked source chunks.
- Added **Ask Aarohi**, a contextual tutor available inside the AI classroom.
- The tutor prioritizes the current segment plus the most relevant lesson segments, returns a concise explanation and a follow-up understanding check, and preserves source citations.
- Added a safe lesson-based fallback so questions still receive an answer when the external AI provider is unavailable.
- Added spoken tutor answers through the same selected teacher voice.
- Added `0.8x`, `1x`, `1.1x`, and `1.25x` voice-speed controls.

## Acceptance checks

- A learner can type a question or use a suggested question and receive a validated response.
- Pressing Enter submits; Shift+Enter creates a new line.
- Moving to a new segment clears the previous tutor conversation.
- The answer shows whether it came from live AI or lesson knowledge and whether source grounding is available.
- The learner can hear the tutor answer and can change the speaking speed before playback.
