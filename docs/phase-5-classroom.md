# Phase 5: AI classroom experience

Phase 5 turns every validated lesson plan into a playable, avatar-led class without requiring a paid video or text-to-speech service.

## Delivered

- Human-like AI teacher portrait generated specifically for Edukriti
- Free browser-native English, Hindi, and Hinglish speech playback
- Live sentence captions driven by speech boundary events
- Pause, resume, replay, previous, next, and direct segment navigation
- Subject-aware equation, code, graph, and concept visuals
- Lesson timeline, progress indicator, objectives, and checkpoint previews
- Graceful caption-only fallback when browser speech is unavailable

## Runtime and safety

Speech is produced entirely by the browser through the Web Speech API. No voice credential is shipped to the browser. Lesson content remains validated against the shared contracts before it reaches the classroom player.

## Next phase

Phase 6 will make checkpoints interactive, evaluate learner answers, identify misconceptions, and adapt the next explanation.
